From initial project design through system installation, we bring each part of your fire protection project together. Large or small, commercial or residential, your needs guide our work.

We are proud partners of Local Union 692 and Local Union 669, with owners bringing over 75 years of combined industry experience.

Do you work on residential and commercial projects?

Yes. Preferred Fire Protection handles projects of all sizes, from commercial buildings to residential properties.

Can you coordinate with our design team?

Yes. Our layout technicians offer CAD drawings and 3D BIM coordination, with REVIT files available when required.

Do you offer inspection and testing contracts?

Yes. Contact our office to discuss an inspection and testing contract for your property.

How do I request emergency service?

Call (302) 256-0607 for 24-hour emergency service. For an active fire or immediate danger, call 911 first.
