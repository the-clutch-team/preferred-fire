Preferred / Services

From the first drawing.
To lasting protection.

Expertise at every stage of your fire protection project.

01

Estimating & planning

Our estimating team helps you explore options for your fire sprinkler needs from the initial inception of your project. We provide competitive pricing for projects of every size.

02

Design & engineering

Our NICET-certified layout technicians provide CAD drawings, project coordination through 3D BIM modeling, and REVIT files when required. Our design experience includes automatic fire sprinklers, fire pumps, underground piping, standpipes, and special hazard systems.

03

Installation

Our union-trained, certified installers deliver professional workmanship in a safe, clean manner. They understand the workings of all types of fire sprinkler devices and apparatus.

04

Inspection & testing

Inspection and testing contracts help you plan the ongoing care of your fire protection system. Contact our team to discuss the system at your property and arrange service.

05

24-hour emergency service

When your fire protection system needs urgent attention, call (302) 256-0607. Emergency service is available 24 hours a day. For an active fire or immediate danger, call 911 first.
